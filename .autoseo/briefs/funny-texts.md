## Angle

The search is for funny texts to _send_, not screenshots of text fails. Treat it as a practical guide: what makes a text land as funny rather than try-hard, copy-adaptable examples grouped by situation (first message after a match, reviving a dead chat, replying to a dry answer, self-deprecating recoveries), and the failure modes — joke stacking, punching down, humour that needs a setup paragraph. Keep the examples short enough to actually send.
