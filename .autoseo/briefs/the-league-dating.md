invite-only with a waitlist, which means the single highest-stakes thing
about it is how your profile reads to a screener — that is the natural
bridge back to profile work, not a bolted-on pitch.

positioning, LinkedIn-linked signup, small daily batch of prospects),
who gets in and who sits on the waitlist, what the paid tiers buy you,
how the dating pool compares with Tinder, and an honest verdict on who
should bother. Do not oversell it and do not trash it — the existing
reviews on this site land because they are even-handed.
