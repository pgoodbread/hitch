## Angle notes

Roast (roast.dating) is an AI-plus-human dating-profile review service: you upload photos, it scores them and suggests which to keep, cut or reshoot. Readers searching `roast dating` are deciding whether to pay for it. The post must be a genuinely honest verdict — what it does well (fast photo scoring, blunt feedback, cheap entry price), where it falls short (AI scores are not the same as match data, bio and opener advice is thinner than the photo side, upsells), and who it is and is not for. Do not write a hit piece and do not write an advert. Give the reader enough to self-diagnose whether a paid review is worth it at all, and name the free things they should fix first.
