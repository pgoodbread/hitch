## Angle & differentiation (important — the site already covers adjacent ground)

This article is about questions you ask **once you're actually dating or on a date** — the
first-date table, the second date, the stage where you're deciding whether this goes
anywhere. It is NOT a crush-stage or opener article. Three existing pages own those
angles and must not be duplicated:

- `/blog/question-to-ask-your-crush` — pre-dating, someone you like but aren't dating
- `/blog/tinder-openers` — the very first message, before any conversation exists
- `/blog/what-to-talk-about-with-a-girl` — general conversation topics, not question lists

Keep this page squarely on **dates and early dating**: what to ask in person, how to ask
it so it doesn't feel like an interview, and what the answers actually tell you. Group the
questions by stage or purpose (first-date icebreakers, questions that reveal values,
questions that build attraction, questions to avoid) rather than dumping a numbered list
of 100. Explain _why_ a question works, not just the question — that is the site's voice
and it is what the competitor page lacks.
