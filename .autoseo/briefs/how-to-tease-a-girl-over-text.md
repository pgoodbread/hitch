## Editorial angle

The keyword is a technique question, not a line-shopping question — but most searchers
arrive wanting lines. Give them both: the mechanic first (what a tease actually is —
a small, low-stakes challenge that signals interest without supplication), then worked
examples they can adapt. Cover the failure mode explicitly: teasing that reads as an
insult, teasing a stranger before any rapport exists, and teasing that never converts
into a date. Distinguish teasing from negging — the site already has `/blog/negging`
arguing why that backfires, so stay consistent: teasing is playful and reciprocal,
negging is a status move. Do not contradict that page.
