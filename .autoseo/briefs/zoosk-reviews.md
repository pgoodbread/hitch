## Angle

An honest, specific verdict on Zoosk: what it actually is in 2026, who it works for, what the paid tier really buys, and where it disappoints. No affiliate-style hype, no fake "we tested it for 90 days" theatre. Prices change; describe the pricing _shape_ (tiered monthly subscription that gets cheaper per month on longer terms, plus a separate coin currency for boosts and Super Sends) rather than quoting exact dollar figures.
