## Search context

"Dating me is like…" is a fill-in-the-blank bio/prompt line. The searcher is mid-way through writing their profile and wants answers they can paste or adapt. They are NOT looking for dating theory — they want a list that works, plus enough explanation to pick one that fits them. The competitor page frames this as a Hinge prompt; our angle is the Tinder bio line, where there is no prompt scaffolding and the line has to carry itself. That difference is the article's edge — say it out loud.

Cover, roughly in this order:

- What the line actually is and why it works (self-deprecating specificity beats brag)
- The formula behind a good answer (concrete noun + unexpected twist, not an adjective)
- A large tagged bank of answers — funny, self-aware, sincere, nerdy, outdoorsy — each with a one-line note on who it fits
- The answers that get you left on read (generic "a rollercoaster", anything negging, anything that reads as a warning label)
- How to place it in a Tinder bio: as the opening line vs. buried mid-bio, and pairing it with the rest of the profile
