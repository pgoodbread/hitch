## Angle

Most "questions to ask your crush" listicles dump 100 generic questions. Beat that by teaching
_why_ certain questions get replies: open-ended over yes/no, specific over generic, hooked to
something in their profile, and escalating in depth rather than opening at maximum intensity.
Still give plenty of concrete examples (grouped by situation: over text, early on, getting
deeper, flirty, before a first date) — but each group should carry a short line on when to use
it and what a good reply looks like. Include what to do when a question lands flat.
