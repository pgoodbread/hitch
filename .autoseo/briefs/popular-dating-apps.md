## Angle

The reader is app-shopping: they are on Tinder (or just off it), it isn't
working, and they want to know which of the popular dating apps is worth
their time. Serve that honestly — what each app is actually good at, who
it suits, where it disappoints — and then land the point the whole site
exists for: the profile travels with you. A bad profile is bad on every
app, and switching is the most common way people avoid fixing it.

Do not write a ranked "top 10" listicle of affiliate blurbs. Group the
popular dating apps by what they're for (swipe-volume apps, slower
prompt-based apps, niche and intent-specific apps, paid/curated apps) and
be concrete about the trade-offs. Name real apps plainly; no scores, no
star ratings, no invented user counts or revenue figures — if a number
isn't something you're sure of, describe the pattern instead.
