## Why this topic

PhotoEval is an AI photo-rating tool for dating profiles — the same problem space
this site sells into. People searching the brand are already convinced their photos
are the bottleneck and are shopping for a way to fix them. The only competitor
ranking for the term is a rival tool's own review page, so an honest third-party
verdict has a clear opening.

## Angle & must-cover

- Lead with a plain answer to "what is photoeval and is it worth it" in the first 100 words.
- Cover: what the tool actually does, how the scoring works in practice, what it costs,
  what it is genuinely good at, and where a score misleads you (a photo can rate well
  in isolation and still fail inside a full profile).
- Be honest and specific rather than promotional. Do not invent pricing tiers, feature
  names, or statistics — if an exact figure is not verifiable, describe the mechanic
  instead of quoting a number.
- Close with how to act on a rating: what to change, what to ignore, and when a second
  opinion on the whole profile beats a per-photo score.
