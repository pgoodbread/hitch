## Angle

Most pages on this keyword are a scrollable dump of 100 copy-paste lines. Beat them on
_why a morning text works_ — it is a low-cost signal of attention that opens a conversation
window, not a greeting. Give the reader a small number of patterns they can adapt (callback
to last night's conversation, a plan-setter, a question with a real answer, a specific
compliment) with examples under each, plus the failure modes: the daily "gm" that becomes
wallpaper, the paragraph that demands too much before coffee, and texting every morning
before there is anything to be warm about. Include a short section on reading the reply —
what an enthusiastic reply, a short reply and silence each actually mean, and what to do next.
