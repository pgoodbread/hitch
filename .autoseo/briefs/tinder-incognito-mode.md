## Angle

Incognito Mode hides you from everyone you haven't swiped right on. Most readers arrive
wanting one of three things: privacy from coworkers/family, a way to lurk without
committing, or a fix for "nobody is seeing me." The article should answer what it is,
who it's actually for, what it costs, how to turn it on and off, and — honestly — the
tradeoff: hiding yourself shrinks your reach, so it is the wrong tool for a matching
problem. Note where it overlaps with and differs from Tinder's older "Show me on Tinder"
toggle and from a shadowban, since readers confuse all three.
