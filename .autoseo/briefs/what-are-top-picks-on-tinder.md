rest of the feature-explainer cluster (Boost, Passport, Incognito Mode, Shadowban, Gold, the

## Angle & must-cover

The competitor page is thin and mostly describes the UI. Beat it by answering the questions people
actually arrive with:

- What Top Picks literally is — the curated daily set Tinder surfaces above the swipe deck, and how
  it differs from the normal deck and from Likes You.
- How many you get free vs. with Gold/Platinum, and how the daily refresh works.
- How Tinder decides who becomes a Top Pick — be honest that Tinder has never published the exact
  inputs. Describe what is actually known (profile completeness, photo quality, stated interests and
  job/education fields feeding the "category" labels like Foodie, Creative, Adventurer) and label
  anything beyond that as inference, not fact. Do not invent a ranking formula.
- Whether being someone's Top Pick means they are more likely to match — and the honest caveat that
  a Top Pick slot gets attention but a weak profile still loses the swipe.
- Whether Top Picks is worth paying for, in the site's usual non-hyped register.
- The practical takeaway: the lever a reader actually controls is the profile, not the placement.

Be accurate about mechanics that change often — prefer "at the time of writing" phrasing over hard
numbers you cannot verify, and avoid stating prices in a way that will rot.
