## Angle (read this first)

This is a consent-first guide, not a manipulation guide. The honest answer to the
keyword is mostly about timing, reciprocity and reading a no — and that is also the
answer that actually works, because the failure mode men search this for is asking
too early and killing the thread. Ground it in what happens on Tinder specifically:
an unsolicited or pushy ask gets you unmatched, reported, and shadowbanned, and
Tinder's own rules prohibit sending unsolicited sexual images. Never advise
pressuring, guilt-tripping, negotiating past a no, trading, or any workaround for
someone who said no. If she is hesitant, the advice is to drop it entirely.
Everything here assumes two consenting adults and an already-sexual, two-way thread.
