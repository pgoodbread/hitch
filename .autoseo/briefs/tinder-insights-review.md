## Angle

This is the same species of post as `/blog/is-tinder-gold-worth-it`, `/blog/tinder-boost` and
`/blog/tinder-passport`: an honest review of a Tinder feature, written for a guy deciding whether
it's worth his attention or money. Reader question behind the query: _"Tinder says it can show me
insights about my profile — is that actually useful, and will it tell me why I'm not getting
matches?"_

Cover, honestly:

- What Tinder Insights actually is and where it shows up in the app (verify this before asserting
  specifics — Tinder ships and retires profile-stat features regularly, and it is not uniformly
  available on every account or tier). If availability is patchy or the feature has changed, say so
  plainly rather than describing a version the reader may not have.
- What the numbers do and don't tell you — a stat about swipes or likes is a symptom, not a
  diagnosis. It cannot tell you _which photo_ is costing you matches.
- Who it's worth it for, who it isn't.
- The practical alternative: reading your own results (match rate, open rate, which opener works)
  and fixing the profile itself — photo order, bio, prompts.

Do not invent precise figures, prices or screenshots. Where a specific detail can't be confirmed,
write around it or describe the general behaviour.
