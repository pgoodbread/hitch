## Angle

Upward is a Christian dating app (owned by Match Group). The searcher wants an honest
read: what it is, who it's for, what it costs, whether it's worth downloading. Write a
straight review-style guide — not a Tinder article with Upward bolted on. Earn the
click, then make the profile advice the natural next step: the photo and bio rules that
work on Upward are the same ones that work everywhere, and that is where the internal
links belong.

Cover, at minimum: what Upward is and who owns it · who it's actually for (and who it
isn't) · how the app works (swipe mechanics, prompts, faith-related profile fields) ·
free vs paid tiers and what the paid tier really buys · how the user base compares to
the big apps in size and intent · the honest verdict. Do not invent specific pricing
figures, user counts, or review scores you are not sure of — describe tiers and
tradeoffs qualitatively rather than quoting numbers you cannot verify.
