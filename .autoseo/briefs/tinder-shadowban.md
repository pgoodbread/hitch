## Angle (why this topic, and the honest take)

Most people searching this are not shadowbanned. They have stalled out — no matches for days
or weeks — and "shadowban" is the explanation that feels least like their own fault. The piece
should take that seriously without flattering it: explain what Tinder actually does (real bans,
review limbo, and the ordinary visibility decay that follows low swipe-through rates), give the
reader a short diagnostic they can run themselves, and be direct that the overwhelmingly common
cause is a profile that people scroll past. Do not sell a conspiracy; do not dismiss the reader.
The fix section is where the profile advice lives.
