## Angle

into the message box), but also over text and DMs. The job is to tell them which flirty
GIFs actually land, when to send one instead of a sentence, and the failure modes (the
GIF as a substitute for having something to say, the wink-face reaction GIF, the
dead-meme pull, sending one as an opener before any rapport exists). Keep it concrete:
describe the _kinds_ of GIF that work rather than linking to specific ones, since no
links are allowed in the body.
